## Observations from sources

- Additional critique calls must be compared to equal-cost sampling baselines.

These are reports and methodological inferences from the cited papers, not direct
observations of this project's test subjects.

## Model update

The original framing implicitly asked one mechanism to do two jobs: raise an alarm
before truth is known and verify that an explanation is false. The literature
supports separating them. Internal inconsistency can trigger investigation;
truth-tracking still requires an external reality channel, possibly delayed.

Confidence: moderate for this conceptual separation, because all four methods fit
it but the scan is narrow and not systematic.

## Important blind spot

Consistency is ambiguous. High semantic agreement may mean knowledge, shared
bias, memorized misinformation, or deterministic decoding. Thus “the model says
the same thing repeatedly” is not evidence of truth. A future instrument must
deliberately test stable false narratives rather than only unstable errors.
