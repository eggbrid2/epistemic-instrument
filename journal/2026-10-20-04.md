condition, and behavioral score.

fixtures. Collect no subject responses.

## Model update

## Missing variables and costs

- Opaque IDs provide procedural blinding, not protection against an annotator who
  deliberately accesses the separate manifest.
- The same repository contains packet-generation and scoring code; role separation
  remains organizational rather than cryptographic.
- A deterministic parser may classify a semantically usable malformed answer as
  invalid, but repairing it after observation would create discretion.
- No independent subject or rhetoric annotator is available yet.
