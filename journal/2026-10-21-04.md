# Session 2026-10-21-04

## Starting task

Add a mechanical leakage audit to the E003 prototype and introduce a second
generator/property family without collecting subject data or fitting an alarm.

## Premise check

Adding a second family does not by itself create a held-out-family test. A family
used to notice or repair leakage is development data. With only eight fixtures,
failure to find a shallow predictor would also be weak evidence.

## Action

- Added four `rotate_boundary_weight` fixtures over binary lists of length five.
- Exhaustively executed all 32 inputs per rotation fixture and cross-checked the
  generated function against a separately coded list transformation.
- Crossed trigger and replacement bits so truth depends on their equality rather
  than either parameter alone.
- Balanced true and false labels within narrative and neutral presentations for
  the new family.
- Expanded generator tests from four to eight items, including source-pair and
  parameter-balance assertions.
- Added a deterministic one-feature construction audit over eight shallow
  features.

## Observation

The first audit caught an artifact: explanation word count achieved 6/8 accuracy
and 0.667 balanced accuracy, above the false-only 5/8 and 0.500 baseline. The
narratives had 22–28 words.

All four narratives were then rewritten to exactly 24 whitespace-delimited words.
After repair, every audited feature scored 5/8 accuracy or less; explanation
length collapsed to the majority baseline. Structural features still reached
0.633 balanced accuracy because the affine family is 1/4 true while the rotation
family is 2/4 true. Thus generator identity remains informative even though it
does not improve raw accuracy on this tiny imbalanced set.

All eight oracle labels and expected counterexample counts pass. The public packet
still contains no private label, counterexample, intent, or response fields.

## Model update

The audit was useful as a bug detector, not as validation. It falsified the local
assumption that approximate narrative-length matching was sufficient. Exact word
matching removes the observed cue, while family-level base-rate balance becomes
the next explicit construction requirement.

## Missing variables and costs

- Eight items permit neither reliable leakage estimation nor train/test splitting.
- The second family is now development material, not a legitimate future holdout.
- Raw lexical features were not mined; doing so now would overfit the audit itself.
- Truth is semantically recoverable from code, as intended; “leakage” must remain
  limited to accidental construction cues.
- Both oracle implementations and all narratives share one author.

## Next candidate action

Build a larger truth-balanced development grid for the two known families, freeze
the surface checks, and only then generate a third family as a holdout. Continue
to prohibit subject calls and alarm fitting.
